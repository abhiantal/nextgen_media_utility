## 0.1.0

* Initial release of **NextGen UI Kit** — a futuristic, high-performance Flutter UI component library.
* **Indicators & Loaders**:
  * `FuturisticLoadingIndicator`: Particle orbits, dual neon counter-rotating rings, energy pulse core, radar scanner HUD.
  * `FuturisticLinearLoadingIndicator`: Indeterminate laser sweep, glowing determinate progress with custom gradients.
  * `CustomProgressIndicator`: Linear progress meters with dynamic growth, percentage badges, and custom animation curves.
  * `AdvancedProgressIndicator`: Full circular and semicircular arc meters with rotating neon glows, custom center widgets, and themes.
* **Metrics & KPIs**:
  * `MetricCard`: Executive stat cards with elevated, flat, glass, and gradient variants, trend indicators, and icons.
  * `MetricBadge`: High-impact status pills with presets (`success`, `warning`, `error`, `info`, `live`, `custom`).
  * `PulseIndicator`: Pulsing glowing beacon for live, recording, or online state indications.
  * `TrendIndicator`: Directional delta change indicator with automatic percentage color coding.
  * `MetricIndicatorGroup`: Responsive wrapping and scrolling layouts for metric badges.
* **Inputs & Forms**:
  * `CustomTextField`: Highly customizable input with email, password, search, multiline, and phone presets, clear button, eye toggle, and glassmorphism.
* **Feedback & Overlays**:
  * `AppSnackbar`: Sleek floating toasts with animated timeout progress bars, shake animations, and haptic feedback.
  * `SnackbarHost`: Single-line MaterialApp wrapper for global snackbars without context hassle.
  * `GlobalSyncStatusBanner`: Smart sliding banner for cloud sync and offline state handling with grace periods and auto-retry.
* **Logging**:
  * `AppLogger`: Color-coded, tag-based debugging logger with formatted output, stack traces, and top-level helpers.
